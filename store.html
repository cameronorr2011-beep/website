The strains

Pick the cell, not the SKU

Every bottle is 16 oz of actively dividing culture with 50 mL of f/2, counted the day before it ships. Add 1,000 g of marine salt for $5 when you are mixing new seawater. Order several strains in one cart and it still ships in one box.

2–3 µm · green · the beginner's strain

Nannochloropsis oculata

Small round cells that grow dense in a gallon with f/2. Feeds rotifers, brine shrimp and early larvae — the one to start with.

$15per bottle

4–6 µm · golden-brown · DHA-rich

Isochrysis aff. galbana (T-Iso)

The base feed in most oyster hatcheries. DHA-rich, flagellated and fast to divide, so it stays in the water column with gentle air.

$15per bottle

4–6 µm · DHA + EPA · scallop and mussel work

Pavlova lutheri

DHA and EPA in one cell, which is why scallop and mussel programmes use it. Slower and less forgiving than T-Iso — usually part of the ration.

$15per bottle

4–6 µm · chain diatom · needs silicate

Chaetoceros muelleri

A silica-walled chain diatom for late larvae and post-set. Order the f/2 with Si, or the culture stalls after a couple of divisions.

$15per bottle

3–5 µm · centric diatom · early larvae

Thalassiosira pseudonana (3H)

A small centric diatom that fits early larvae where a chain would be too big. Silicate-dependent, and quick to be outcompeted between batches.

$15per bottle

7–10 µm · green · late larvae and broodstock

Tetraselmis suecica

Large, green and robust — a late-larval and broodstock feed, and a standard rotifer enrichment. Too big for a first-feeding veliger.

$15per bottle

Micrographs are credited on the strain page. Each bottle is live culture, not a paste or a concentrate.

Questions

Before you send the order

The five things people email about. Anything else: service@orrbiologicals.com.

Can I mix strains in one order?

Yes. Add each bottle to the cart separately and the order arrives itemised. Mixed bottles still ship together in one box, in the same Monday-to-Wednesday window.

Is the marine salt sold on its own?

No. The 1,000 g bag is a $5 upgrade with a bottle of culture, because a bag of salt without algae is not a product we can ship usefully. Choose the salt bundle on any strain.

How is shipping calculated?

By address, in our reply. US only. Bottles go out insulated with a cold pack in warm months, and we hold the order rather than ship into a heatwave.

What if the culture arrives dead?

Photograph it within 24 hours and send the photo to service@orrbiologicals.com. It is replaced. That is the whole warranty, and it is the only one that matters with live material.

Do I need an account to order?

No. The cart is saved in your browser, and the order goes by email. There is no password to create and no card details are entered on this site.
