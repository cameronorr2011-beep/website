Start with a bottle, not a case.

Most orders here begin with a 16 oz starter culture: a dense, dividing culture in a sealed bottle, five to seven days old, that seeds about 4.7 L in a week. It is the cheapest way to begin a batch, restart after a crash, or keep a backup strain on the shelf.
