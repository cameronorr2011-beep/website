Start with a tube, not a case.

Most orders here begin with a 50 mL start culture: a dense, dividing culture in a sealed tube, five to seven days old, that becomes 500 mL in a week. It is the cheapest way to begin a batch, restart after a crash, or keep a backup strain on the shelf.

Hatcheries ordering weekly Nannochloropsis should look at batch cultures and standing orders instead. Classrooms and home culturists usually want the $59 kit, which includes a counted starter and everything needed to run it.
