What we do

Three disciplines, one living system

Our work sits at the intersection of biology, engineering, and computation — building systems that make cultivation measurable, controllable, and reproducible.

01

Biological Cultivation

Controlled cultivation systems designed to optimize biological growth and productivity — engineered environments for photosynthetic microorganisms.

02

Microalgae Biotechnology

Research involving organisms such as Spirulina, Chlorella, and Haematococcus, with potential applications across nutrition, materials, biotechnology, and research.

03

Intelligent Biological Systems

Software, sensors, automation, and data analysis designed to make biological cultivation more measurable and controllable.

Research philosophy

Biological systems are complex. We work methodically.

Orr Biologicals combines experimentation with engineering and computational tools — one controlled variable at a time.

01

Measure

Capture culture state continuously with calibrated, validated sensors.

02

Model

Represent the culture with transparent, physics-informed models.

03

Experiment

Change one thing. Observe the response. Record the outcome.

04

Optimize

Use what was learned to propose the next, better condition.

Future vision

Biology should become measurable, programmable, and increasingly autonomous.

These are research directions we find compelling — not products that exist today.
