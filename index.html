01 / What is on the counter

Three cultures,
one habit.

Each one is a small ecosystem you feed and observe. None of them needs a laboratory. All of them reward the same things: clean vessels, steady conditions and a notebook beside the jar. Our research approach explains how we separate an observation from a guess.

Sourdough starter

Flour and water, colonised by wild yeast and lactic acid bacteria. Feed it, watch the rise, and learn what a healthy jar looks and smells like.

02 / How we keep them

Nothing here needs
a lab coat.

Home fermentation is ordinary, forgiving and occasionally wrong. These three habits make it easier to tell the difference between a culture that is working and one that has quietly gone off.

01

Clean, then dry.

Wash jars, lids and spoons in hot soapy water and let them dry. You do not need a sterile field; a clean, dry vessel is enough for a home culture.

02

Keep conditions steady.

Most failures are a change in temperature, feeding or airflow. Keep the jar out of direct sun, away from draughts, and give it a predictable spot.

03

Write it down.

Date, flour or tea, temperature, what you changed, what you saw. A line a day turns a vague feeling into something you can actually compare.

03 / Advanced technologies

Our inventions,
kept separate.

The kitchen notes are for anyone. This cabinet holds the two instruments we are inventing: one for growing cultures at scale, one for looking at single cells. Both are research concepts in development, not products you can buy.

A–01

Algaephyte

Sensors, cameras and a growth model brought into one clearer view of a changing culture. Illustrative scene: a proposed instrument and its readings, not a deployed instrument.

A–02

Cyanoflow

A proposed way to separate, photograph and follow individual cells so their differences are not averaged away. Design goals and illustrative imagery, not validated hardware performance.

05 / Open by default

Readable,
and reachable.

The site is built to be read by as many people as possible: real headings, keyboard focus that is visible, text that does not depend on colour alone, motion that stops when you ask it to. The site code is MIT-licensed.

If a page is hard to use with your setup, that is a bug. Write to us and we will fix it.
