03 / OUR CURIOSITY

Big questions.
Small beginnings.

A growing culture is full of change. Cells divide. Light shifts. Conditions move. Understanding that small world starts with paying attention.

We bring biology, engineering, and software together to look more closely at life. Our research concepts in development explore how to grow algae thoughtfully and understand the differences between individual cells.

We share the ideas, the questions, and the limits of the work as it develops.
