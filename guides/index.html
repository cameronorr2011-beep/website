How to use the shelf

Start with the culture you actually have. Each guide is written to be read once before you begin and then kept open while you work. The tables are the part worth returning to: they describe what a healthy jar looks like at each stage, and the point at which looking is no longer enough.

The guides are educational notes, not food-safety certification. They deliberately avoid promising a result, because a kitchen is not a controlled chamber. If a guide and your own observation disagree, trust the observation and write it down — that is the habit the whole site is built around.

A note on what is missing. There is no guide here for a culture we have not kept. Where a topic is still being worked out — scale-up, instrumentation, single-cell measurement — it lives under advanced technologies and is labelled as a research concept rather than a recipe.
