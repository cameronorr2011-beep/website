Disclaimer

Last updated September 30, 2026
We built Orr Biologicals to make the science of growing microalgae open to everyone. Home cultivation is rewarding, and with reasonable care it is very approachable. It is also something you do yourself, on your own equipment, with living cultures and food you may choose to eat. This page explains plainly where our role ends and yours begins. None of it is meant to scare you off; it is meant to help you grow responsibly, and safely.

Current project status

Algaephyte and Cyanoflow are research concepts in development, not validated products. The site presents proposed architectures, illustrative imagery and interactive simulations, not completed hardware deployments or measured production results. Earlier platform descriptions should be read as design goals, not demonstrated capabilities. No finished reactor orders or reservations are offered.

Educational information only, not professional advice

Everything on this site, including articles, guides, videos, code, and project documentation, is provided for general educational and informational purposes only. It draws on reading, proposed designs and educational models; it should not be treated as a record of validated Algaephyte or Cyanoflow experiments.

It is not professional, medical, nutritional, engineering, legal, or food-safety advice, and it is not a substitute for guidance from a qualified professional about your specific situation.

Living systems vary. Your strain, water, equipment, climate, and habits are not ours, so your conditions and results may differ from anything described here.

You grow and consume at your own risk

If you choose to cultivate Spirulina and to eat what you grow, you do so entirely at your own risk and on your own judgment.

You, not Orr Biologicals, are solely responsible for deciding whether anything you produce is safe to consume.

If you are ever unsure whether a culture is healthy, correctly identified, or safe, the answer is simple: do not eat it.

Food safety is your responsibility

Home food production carries real responsibilities. You are solely responsible for correctly identifying your strain, keeping your culture free of contamination, and recognizing the warning signs of a crash or an unwanted organism, such as a pH that falls unexpectedly alongside a foul smell.

Spirulina is grown in a highly alkaline medium containing salts such as sodium bicarbonate. You are responsible for harvesting on appropriate mesh and rinsing those alkaline salts off the biomass before any culinary use.

Fresh Spirulina paste is perishable. You are responsible for proper harvesting, rinsing, refrigeration, prompt use, and low-temperature drying, and for discarding anything that looks, smells, or tastes off. When in doubt, throw it out.

We cannot inspect your culture or your kitchen, and we accept no responsibility for anything you consume.

No medical or health claims

Nothing on this site is intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or health condition, and nothing here should be read as a promise of any health, nutritional, or therapeutic benefit.

We make no medical claims about Spirulina. Descriptions of its composition are general educational background, not health advice.

Consult a qualified physician or dietitian before adding any home-grown food to your diet, especially if you are pregnant, nursing, have a medical condition, take medication, or have allergies. Individual reactions vary.

Electrical, water, and equipment safety

Algaephyte and its associated hardware are do-it-yourself electronics that operate around water, pumps, heaters, and mains-adjacent power. Mixing electricity and water is inherently hazardous.

If you build, wire, or run any of this equipment, you are responsible for doing so safely and correctly: proper enclosures, grounding, residual-current or GFCI protection, appropriate cabling, and compliance with local electrical codes. If you are not confident, consult a qualified electrician.

You are responsible for supervising heaters and pumps, preventing overflow and spills, and keeping the physical emergency-stop within reach. Automation is an aid, not a babysitter.

Algaephyte and Algaephyte Mesh are experimental and provided as is

Algaephyte, its digital twin, and the Algaephyte Mesh federated-learning network are experimental, open-source, non-commercial projects under active development. They may contain bugs, make imperfect decisions, or fail.

The safety features, including deterministic hard limits, digital-twin checks, firmware clamps, and the physical e-stop, are designed to fail closed and to reduce risk, but they are not guaranteed and cannot make an inherently unsafe setup safe. The system only ever proposes actions; the final responsibility for your reactor is yours.
