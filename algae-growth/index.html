SCENE 01

Start with a viable cell

A small inoculum begins with water, dissolved carbon dioxide, light, and mineral nutrients. Each cell is a complete living system with its own boundary, pigments, and machinery for turning energy into new material.

light+CO₂+H₂O+nutrients→biomass + O₂

The exact pigments, shape, and division rhythm vary by species and conditions.
