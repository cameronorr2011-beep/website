Explore the world of microalgae

By Orr Biologicals · August 31, 2026 · 2 min read

Algaephyte began as a Spirulina reactor. The questions it kept asking — how does this culture actually behave, what does the model predict, is it safe to act — turned out to be questions about microalgae and phytoplankton generally. This library collects what we have learned and researched: how microalgae are cultivated, how they feed aquaculture from copepods to fish larvae, what each major species is good at, and where the biotechnology goes.

Everything here is written to be useful before it is promotional. Where parameters vary by species or strain, we say so. Where the evidence is thin, we say that too.
