Applications · Evidence-led dossiers

The mechanism, the evidence, the limits.

Applications is where biology meets a job to be done. Every dossier here states the biological mechanism, what research has actually demonstrated, what has not, and what it would take — with primary literature, clearly attributed. Where a domain is only ready to be mapped, the dossier says so instead of performing depth it does not have.

This division Applications explains real problems and the evidence around them — if you want to build something on your bench, that is DIY; the literature index lives under Articles; our own engineering lives under Inventions.

01 · Aquaculture biotechnology Live dossier

Phytoplankton for bivalve hatcheries

The problem: bivalve larvae only eat live microalgae, and hatcheries live or die on feed quality and stability. The mechanism: species, cell size and fatty-acid profile matched to larval stage. The evidence: established hatchery practice (FAO Fisheries Technical Paper 471). The limits: strain-dependent; not validated by Orr Biologicals; educational starting point, not a licensed hatchery protocol.

02 · Advanced microbiology & bioprocessing Evidence map

Microbial cultivation systems & process monitoring

The mechanism: selection pressure, carbon and nitrogen management, gas exchange and mixing, monitored honestly. The evidence: lab-scale photobioreactor practice (Schagerl 2022; Benner 2022) and our cultivation pillar and reactor engineering explainer. The limits: scale-up alters illumination and transport; no Orr Biologicals process is validated; see research approach.

03 · Spider silk & biological materials Dossier in development

Recombinant silk proteins & biological fiber engineering

The mechanism: spidroins combine crystalline strength with amorphous elasticity; recombinant expression (e.g. in E. coli) and protein assembly are the active engineering levers. The evidence: literature map only — start with Lewis, “Spider silk: the unraveling of a mystery,” Accounts of Chemical Research, 2006, and Kluge et al., “Spider silks and their applications,” Trends in Biotechnology, 2008. The limits: this page maps the field; it does not present Orr Biologicals results, protocols or products. A sourced dossier is written before it is published.

○

04 · Environmental biotechnology Dossier in development

Microalgae and nutrient recovery

The mechanism: microalgae fix nitrogen and phosphorus from wastewater streams while producing harvestable biomass — the same organism treated as a nutrient sink and as a product. The evidence: literature map only — see Christenson & Sims, “Production and harvesting of microalgae for wastewater treatment,” Biotechnology Advances, 2011. The limits: coupling ecology to engineered systems is hard; nothing here is a treatment system, and no removal percentage is claimed.

○
A note on dossier maturity. A dossier ships when it can carry its claims: mechanism, evidence, limits and sources, or it stays labelled as a map. Aspiration is not evidence — the same rule we apply on bench protocols.

Citations here are indexes into the literature, not verified measurements by Orr Biologicals. Verify before you rely on them; literature search checks a DOI against public records.
