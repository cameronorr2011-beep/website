What every protocol here promises

  1. Repeatability. Steps a careful reader can follow on day one — quantities, temperatures, timing, and what to log.
  2. Credited sources. Claims tie back to named literature or to our own field notes; nothing is asserted from nowhere.
  3. Honest limits. Every page states what it cannot certify — identity, food safety, legal compliance — and where that line sits.
  4. Open licence. The site, notes and build scripts are MIT-licensed. Fork this bench; we would like to see what you learn.

How this section connects

Applications are the home bench. The research approach explains how we separate an observation from a guess; the guide shelf collects every protocol in one searchable place; Play offers three small puzzles built from the same material. The advanced instruments — our inventions, Algaephyte and Cyanoflow — live separately, and are listed under Inventions.

Educational, not certification. Nothing here is food-safety certification, veterinary advice, aquaculture licensing or a recipe for unregulated release. When in doubt, discard the batch — and read the safety line on the page you are using.
