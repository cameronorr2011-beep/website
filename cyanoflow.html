Cyanoflow

A single-cell discovery concept for microalgae and cyanobacteria. Compartmentalize cells in a microfluidic chip, image them over time, extract measurable features, and let a model surface the individuals worth investigating.

The point is resolution: instead of measuring the average of a culture, Cyanoflow turns biological diversity into searchable data.

The pipeline

From a drop of culture to a ranked list of individual cells

Each stage is a separable engineering problem. Stages 06 and 08 are shown live further down the page.

01

Sample

A prepared microalgal sample enters the system. Real environmental material contains many different cells and particles, so the first stage exists to make the input predictable enough for controlled analysis: filtration, dilution to a target cell density, and a defined suspension medium.

  • Approved, well-characterized strain
  • Filtered / diluted to target density
  • Buffer + surfactant compatibility checked
  • No casual culturing of unknown environmental microbes
02

Single-cell compartmentalization

The sample enters a microfluidic chip whose microscopic channels carry the liquid. One approach is to pinch the aqueous stream into tiny droplets surrounded by an immiscible carrier fluid, so each droplet becomes an isolated compartment. The objective is to create compartments containing exactly one cell often enough for single-cell analysis to be practical.

sample in →→ imaging
○ empty compartment● single cell◉ doublet — discarded

Loading follows Poisson statistics, so you deliberately run dilute: many empty droplets is the price of a high fraction of single-cell droplets. The cell is never glued down — it stays suspended in its own microscopic liquid environment.

03

Imaging

The compartments move through an imaging region where a microscope and camera capture repeatedly. Instead of one photograph per cell, Cyanoflow is built around time-series observation — the same compartment seen at t0…tn. That is what lets you measure behaviour, not just appearance.

04

Computer vision

Raw microscope frames are not useful to a model until they become measurable information. Segmentation and tracking convert pixels into per-cell numbers across four families: morphology, growth, optics and behaviour. The unit of storage stops being a file name and becomes a record.

05

AI phenotyping

The model takes those measurements and searches for structure — clusters, outliers, and combinations of features that track with a phenotype you care about. Ten thousand cells collapse into a ranked shortlist you can actually work through by hand.

The model never gets to declare "this cell is good." You define what interesting means as a measurable rule — e.g. a combination of growth behaviour and optical phenotype — and the model finds cells that satisfy it.

07

Candidate recovery

Once a promising compartment is identified you want it physically separated from the rest for downstream characterisation — regrowth, sequencing, biochemical assay. This is where Cyanoflow stops being an analysis instrument and becomes a discovery platform.

Routing a specific compartment — dielectrophoretic or valve-based deflection triggered by the classifier — is the hardest subsystem. A first prototype can stop at reliable imaging, tracking and classification and still be a substantial project.

09

What makes it different

The difference is not the microscope and it is not the model. It is the resolution of the biological search: you are converting biological diversity into searchable data, then querying it.

Conventional
  1. culture
  2. measure the average
  3. adjust conditions
  4. repeat

Population resolution. Rare biology is averaged away.

Cyanoflow
  1. individual cells
  2. measure each one
  3. identify unusual phenotypes
  4. investigate candidates

Single-cell resolution. Diversity becomes searchable.

Safety framing

Approved, well-characterised strains only; standard containment and disposal; no culturing of unknown environmental microbes.

11 · Long-term vision

A high-throughput biological discovery engine

Instead of manually examining hundreds of cells, the system processes very large numbers of individuals — and every pass makes the next selection sharper.

Orr Biologicals · platform handoff

Cyanoflow

Discovery

Finds interesting biology. Single-cell compartmentalization, time-series imaging, feature extraction, phenotyping, candidate recovery and characterisation.

characterised strain + measured biology

Algaephyte

Cultivation

Figures out how to grow it intelligently. Controlled cultivation, condition optimisation, production scale-up — fed by the phenotypes Cyanoflow surfaces.

Keeping the two technologies distinct is what makes the platform coherent: one machine that searches biology, another that grows it — instead of one machine trying to do everything simultaneously.

The platform represents a developing research concept combining microfluidics, microscopy, computer vision, and machine learning for single-cell microalgal research.

Capabilities shown on this website — including every simulation and interactive console on this page — should be presented as design goals, prototypes, or research objectives unless experimentally validated.

For environmental or unknown microorganisms, experimentation will follow applicable biosafety, institutional, and science-fair requirements.
