What's in the box, and how the first week goes

What arrives, and what to do with it

No account, no minimum, no subscription. You send an email with your address, I send a bottle back.

The bottle

473 mL of dense green culture, sealed and packed on a Monday, Tuesday or Wednesday. It's one strain, not a blend: Nannochloropsis oculata, 2–3 µm, counted before it leaves. Keep it cool and out of direct sun until you use it.

The food

50 mL of f/2 at 500×. Two milliliters per liter of culture water, which is about 15 drops in a 16 oz bottle. One bottle carries you through roughly 25 liters, so it outlasts the culture it came with.

Growing it on

Mix salt water at 30 ppt, add the f/2, pour in the culture, and give it light and a little movement. Twelve hours of light, twelve off. Split it weekly if you want to keep it going; the kit page walks through the first week.

Shipping

Monday to Wednesday only, US addresses only. Live arrival is guaranteed: if it arrives dead, send a photo and you get a replacement or a refund. Heat and cold are the usual reasons a box fails, which is why the shipping days are narrow.
