What “counted before it ships” means

  • Haemocytometer count. Four grids, averaged, recorded as cells per mL on the label. A culture below 1.5 × 107 does not ship — it is held and re-grown, not sold as a full batch.
  • Colour and motility. Deep olive-green with no yellowing at the meniscus. Flagellates are checked for swimming under 100×. Pale means nitrogen-starved; brown means it is on its way out.
  • Contamination screen. Any cell that is not the strain is a fail: ciliates, cyanobacteria filaments, diatom chains in a flagellate culture. Failures are discarded, not discounted, and the vessel is sterilised.

Packing and the last mile

  • Monday to Wednesday only. Priority Mail, one to three days. Nothing leaves on a Thursday or Friday: a culture that sits in a sorting facility over a weekend arrives clear and dead.
  • Packed for heat. Double bagged with an absorbent pad, two layers of bubble wrap, a gel pack separated from the culture by an inch of air, and a red live-culture label. Ground service is not used at all.
  • We will refuse a heatwave. Above 32 °C at the destination the order is held and you get an email, not a box. Below 4 °C the same applies with a heat pack. A day's delay beats a replacement shipment.
  • Live arrival. Photograph the unopened vessel within 24 hours of delivery and it is replaced. That is the entire warranty, and it is unconditional.

Questions from both ends of the bench

The ones that arrive with the first email. Anything else, ask directly — a technical answer costs nothing.

What will a 16 oz starter culture grow?

A 16 oz bottle holds 473 mL of culture at roughly 2 × 107 cells per mL. Diluted 1:9 into seawater with f/2 it seeds about 4.7 L at 2 × 106 cells per mL, which is enough to run a litre of working culture and split it the following week. The step-by-step is here.

Can a hobbyist or a classroom order here, or is this hatchery-only?

Anyone can order. The 16 oz starter and a bottle of f/2 are the two bench-sized products, and the $59 culture kit bundles a 50 mL starter with the vessel, salt, air and light for someone starting from nothing. There is no account and no minimum order; the only difference from a hatchery order is the volume.

What cell density do the batch cultures ship at?

Nannochloropsis oculata ships at roughly 2 × 107 cells per mL after seven days of growth, which is deliberate. Denser cultures survive shipping worse than dilute ones, so the culture arrives still dividing rather than stalled.

Can I set up a standing weekly order?

Yes. Standing orders are scheduled a month at a time and priced per litre per week, below one-off pricing. Tell us the volume and the day you need it and the batch is grown to that date, not pulled from stock.

Do you supply silicate for diatom cultures?

Yes. Standard f/2 omits silicate because it invites diatom contamination in flagellate cultures. For Chaetoceros and Thalassiosira orders we supply an f/2 plus silicate variant, dosed the same way.

Can you supply a strain you do not list?

Often, if it is a marine species we can obtain clean and confirm. Strain isolation and scale-up is quoted per project with a two to six week lead time depending on the source material. Send the species name and what you are feeding it to.
