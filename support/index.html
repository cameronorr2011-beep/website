Where it goes

What contributions help support

Independent research work at Orr Biologicals currently runs on development and measurement models described on the research methodology page and the research journal. Contributions help fund continuing development of the Algaephyte monitoring concepts, the Cyanoflow single-cell observation designs, the Algal Digital Lab simulation workspace, open literature-discovery tooling, and clearly labeled educational research notes and glossary pages.

No specific deliverable is promised. Development direction is published honestly as it happens, with experimental limitations stated.
