A full stack for living systems

Hardware that senses, software that records, and models that make biology more legible — built open and verified at every step.

The control loop

Culture → Sensors → Data → Model → Decision → Control → Culture

CultureSensorsDataModelDecisionControlCulture

Every action is bounded by a deterministic safety gate before it reaches the hardware. The loop is a closed cycle: the outcome of each action feeds the next observation.

Hardware

Instruments for culture state

Modular sensing and actuation hardware built around a single-board computer, designed to be repaired and extended.

Sensors

Sensing

Optical density, pH, temperature, light, and CO₂ probes — the continuous measurement layer.

Actuation

Lighting & pumps

Tunable LED illumination and peristaltic pumps for nutrient dosing.

Gas & climate

CO₂ & environment

Gas supply and environmental monitoring for stable cultivation.

Compute

Controllers

Single-board computer running on-device models; optional Edge TPU for vision.

Vision

Cameras

On-device imaging for culture morphology and contamination cues.

Safety

Protection

Hardware limits, watchdogs, and a physical stop — fail-closed by design.

Software

From raw signal to experiment record

Open-source software that turns measurements into structured, queryable experiment history.

Data

Telemetry & logging

Continuous capture of sensor readings with validation and time-stamping.

Interface

Visualization

Dashboards and charts that make culture trajectory legible at a glance.

Science

Modeling

Digital-twin simulations of growth kinetics and culture dynamics.

Process

Experiment tracking

Structured records linking conditions, actions, and outcomes.

Control

Automated control

Decision logic that proposes bounded actions, verified twice before actuation.

Privacy

Local-first

Experiment data stored on the device by default; nothing uploaded without consent.

Biological intelligence

Measurements, meeting models

The long-term direction: measurements feed computational models that help identify favorable cultivation conditions.

Today this means transparent digital twins and safety-checked automation. The vision is models that grow more useful with every experiment — always with a human reviewing the decisions.
