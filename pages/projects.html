Research systems, built in the open

Two working prototype systems form the core of our research. Neither is for sale — both are under active development, and their designs and software are open source.

Research Prototype

Algaephyte

Spirulina photobioreactor · on-device intelligence

Algaephyte is a photobioreactor system for Spirulina cultivation. It measures culture state continuously, runs a digital-twin model on-device, and proposes bounded control actions that pass a two-layer safety gate before any actuator moves.

Biological system
Spirulina (Arthrospira)
Technology
Raspberry Pi 4 · Coral Edge TPU · optical, pH, temperature, light & CO₂ sensing
Software
Digital twin · safety-gated control · local experiment history
Status
Research prototype — not available for sale
License
MIT (open source)

Research Prototype

Mycophyte

Mushroom fruiting chamber · microclimate + twin

Mycophyte is a controlled fruiting chamber for mushroom cultivation. It manages microclimate — humidity, air exchange, temperature, and lighting — and applies the same digital-twin and safety-gate architecture used in Algaephyte.

Biological system
Edible fungi (fruiting stage)
Technology
Raspberry Pi 4 · Coral Edge TPU · microclimate sensing & actuation
Software
Digital twin · safety-gated control · vision-based assessment
Status
Research prototype — not available for sale

MIT (open source)
