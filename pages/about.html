A small lab with a large ambition

Orr Biologicals is an independent research effort focused on making biological systems easier to understand, measure, and engineer.

Our mission

Practical technologies for living systems

Develop practical technologies that make biological systems easier to understand, measure, and engineer.

Our approach

Biology, engineering, computation

We combine biology, engineering, computation, and experimentation — building open systems and testing them honestly, one variable at a time.

Development timeline

Where we've been, so far

Factual milestones only. Orr Biologicals is early-stage; this timeline will grow as the work does.

Phase 01

Initial concept

Exploration of open, low-cost cultivation systems and the sensing needed to make cultures measurable.

Phase 02

First prototypes

Working photobioreactor and fruiting-chamber prototypes with continuous sensing and local data logging.

Phase 03

Open-source publication

Software and design files published under the MIT license — the projects page lists the current systems.

Now

Prototype refinement

Improving sensors, models, and safety-gated automation; preparing more rigorous experimental evaluation.
