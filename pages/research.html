Research programs, in progress

Every program below is early-stage. Development stages are stated honestly — nothing here is validated, commercial, or published.

Stage key

How to read a stage

Exploring

Direction identified; first concepts and literature review.

Prototype

Working hardware or software exists in a test configuration.

Experimental

Under active measurement; results are preliminary and unvalidated.

In Development

Being prepared for broader use; not yet released or offered.

Program 01

Microalgae cultivation

Scientific objectiveEstablish reproducible cultivation of Spirulina (Arthrospira) and related strains under controlled laboratory conditions.
Development stagePrototype
Organisms / systemsSpirulina (Arthrospira platensis) in photobioreactor vessels; candidate strains for later work include Chlorella and Haematococcus.
Current workGrowth medium formulation, culture maintenance, and optical monitoring of culture health.
Potential applicationsNutrition, research platforms, aquaculture feed, and biomass production.
Program 02

Photobioreactor engineering

Scientific objectiveDesign open-source reactors that control light, mixing, temperature, pH, and gas exchange for photosynthetic cultures.
Development stagePrototype
SystemsColumn-type photobioreactor with LED illumination, gas sparging, and sensor ports; open hardware documentation.
Current workReactor geometry, light distribution, mixing behavior, and sensor integration.
Potential applicationsResearch cultivation, distributed production, and education.
Program 03

Biological sensing

Scientific objectiveMeasure culture state continuously and non-invasively: biomass, pH, temperature, light, and CO₂.
Development stagePrototype
SystemsOptical density measurement, pH probe, temperature sensing, light sensing, and CO₂ monitoring.
Current workSensor calibration, data validation, and rejection of unreliable readings.
Potential applicationsCulture monitoring across research and production settings.
Program 04

Biological modeling

Scientific objectiveBuild transparent digital-twin models that forecast growth and culture trajectory from sensor data.
Development stageExperimental
SystemsDroop-type nutrient quota models, light-response kinetics, and self-shading corrections.
Current workModel calibration against observed culture trajectories; forecast accuracy is not yet established.
Potential applicationsHarvest timing, experiment design, and condition optimization.
Program 05

Automation & data analysis

Scientific objectiveAutomate routine cultivation control and turn logged data into usable experiment records.
Development stageExperimental
SystemsActuator control with a deterministic safety gate; local experiment history and telemetry logging.
Current workSafe control of lighting and dosing; validation of logged data for later analysis.
Potential applicationsUnattended research cultivation and reproducible experiment protocols.
