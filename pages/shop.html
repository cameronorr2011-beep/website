The shop

What we grow, print & ship

Everything here is open-source first — the plans, firmware and schematics are free. This shop exists to save you time: printed parts, sensor kits, and cultures for the AlgaePhyte and Mycophyte builds. Nothing is listed until it exists and is tested.

AlgaePhyte sensor kit

pH probe + adapter, DS18B20, BH1750, peristaltic pump heads, and the ESP32-CAM microscope mount. Everything except the Pi and the Coral USB Accelerator, which you source yourself.

Status: in testing · waitlist open

Mycophyte chamber kit

DHT22, MH-Z19B CO₂ sensor, TSL2591, relay board, humidifier + fan mounting brackets for a standard 60L tub.

Status: in testing · waitlist open

Printed parts pack

The full set of 3D-printable brackets, shrouds and mounts from both HARDWARE.md files — PETG, with free STL files alongside if you print your own.

Status: in testing · waitlist open

Culture & spawn

Arthrospira (Spirulina) starter cultures for the photobioreactor, and mycelium spawn syringes for the chamber. Cultured per order — freshness over speed.

Status: soon · waitlist open
