Privacy Policy

Last updated September 8, 2026

This website is a static site. It does not run advertising, does not sell data, and does not share your information with third parties for marketing.

Information we collect

Contact form. If you use the pilot-access form, the information you submit (name, place, what you grow, message) is used solely to respond to your inquiry. Until a form service is configured, the form does not transmit data to a server — it validates locally and offers a mailto link. Your email client and our mailbox handle the message you choose to send.

Server logs. Like most websites, our host may record standard server logs (such as IP address, requested pages, and timestamps) for security and operational purposes. We do not use these to profile visitors.

Cookies and local storage

We keep non-essential storage strictly opt-in. This site uses:

  • Strictly necessary storage. A record of your cookie-choice (ob-cookie-consent) so we don't ask you on every visit. This always runs because it is required to honor your choice.
  • Preference storage (consent-gated). The Algae Living game saves progress, settings, and your best-run data in your browser's local storage (algae-living-save). This only starts after you accept optional storage; if you decline, the game still runs, it just won't save your progress.

We do not use advertising cookies, social-media pixels, cross-site trackers, or fingerprinting. Nothing here follows you around the web. You can clear the game save and the consent record at any time via your browser's "clear site data" or by using the "Reset" option in the game itself.

Legal basis

For visitors in the UK/EU: the strictly necessary choice-record is used under legitimate interest (remembering your consent choice); game preferences are used only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by declining or clearing the entry.

Children

The site is educational and written to be family-safe, but it is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

You may ask what personal information we hold about you (in practice: only correspondence you sent us), ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw consent for optional storage. Email us and we will respond promptly.

Changes to this policy

If we ever add a real analytics service, a payment flow, or anything else that changes what is collected, we will update this page and the date at the top before that goes live.

Contact

Questions about this policy: service@orrbiologicals.com
