Privacy Policy

Last updated September 8, 2026

This website is a static site. It does not run advertising, does not sell data, and does not share your information with third parties for marketing.

Information we collect

Contact form. If you use the research-correspondence form, the information you submit (name, organization, location, and objective) is used solely to respond to your inquiry. The form does not transmit data to a server — it validates locally and offers a mailto link. Your email client and our mailbox handle the message you choose to send.

Server logs. Like most websites, our host may record standard server logs (such as IP address, requested pages, and timestamps) for security and operational purposes. We do not use these to profile visitors.

Cookies and local storage

We keep non-essential storage strictly opt-in. This site uses:

  • Strictly necessary storage. A record of your cookie-choice (ob-cookie-consent) so we don't ask you on every visit. This always runs because it is required to honor your choice.
  • Research workspace storage. The Algal Digital Lab can keep project drafts, runs, and notebook entries in a namespaced browser-local record. It is not an authenticated account. The workspace uses an optional remote endpoint only when you explicitly configure one.

We do not use advertising cookies, social-media pixels, cross-site trackers, or fingerprinting. Nothing here follows you around the web. You can clear the local workspace records and the consent record at any time via your browser's "clear site data" controls.

Legal basis

For visitors in the UK/EU: the strictly necessary choice-record is used under legitimate interest (remembering your consent choice); browser-local research records remain on your device unless you explicitly configure an optional remote endpoint.

Children

The site is educational and written to be family-safe, but it is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

You may ask what personal information we hold about you (in practice: only correspondence you sent us), ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw consent for optional storage. Email us and we will respond promptly.

Changes to this policy

If we ever add a real analytics service, a payment flow, or anything else that changes what is collected, we will update this page and the date at the top before that goes live.

Contact

Questions about this policy: service@orrbiologicals.com
