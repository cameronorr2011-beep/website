A · Build your own equipment

Instrumentation you can afford is the difference between “I think it grew” and “the log says it did.” Honest status, no catalog fiction:

01 · DIY incubators & culture chambers In development

Design notes for an insulated, light-controlled culture chamber and a warming incubator are being written. Nothing is published until a build is documented end-to-end — no dimension set ships without photographs of an actual build.

○

02 · Temperature & monitoring for cultures Reading first

What to measure before you automate anything: temperature drift, light, pH — see reading the culture and the research approach. Build sheets for logging rigs follow the protocols; they are not rushed ahead of them.

○

03 · System-scale instrumentation See inventions

Algaephyte and Cyanoflow — our own proposed cultivation-monitoring and single-cell observation systems. Research concepts in development; not validated hardware, not for sale.
