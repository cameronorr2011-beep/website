The setting

The microscopic world

You live inside a photobioreactor — a glass column of swirling water, light and chemistry. Everything here is alive, edible, or interested in you.

  • Algae — you and your kin
  • Light — free energy
  • Carbon — building material
  • Nitrogen — growth fuel
  • Phosphorus — rare treats
  • Bacteria — complicated neighbors
  • Colonies — safety in numbers
  • Waste — someone's lunch
