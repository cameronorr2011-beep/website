# BioRealm original asset note

The BioRealm scene artwork in `game/js/biorealm-scene.js` and the music and
sound design generated by `game/js/biorealm-audio.js` are original procedural
work created for Orr Biologicals. The illustrations, inline microscopy
portraits, palettes, melodies, synthesis recipes, and generated noise are
not copied from an external asset library and do not require third-party
asset attribution.

The scientific genus names used by the game identify reference organisms.
They do not imply that the organisms, morphology descriptions, or synthetic
game economy are licensed scientific source material. All game behavior and
presentation remain fictional and local to BioRealm.
