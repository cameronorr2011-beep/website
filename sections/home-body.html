03 / THE QUESTION

See the signal before it becomes a claim.

Living systems are not dashboards. They are changing cultures, individual cells, imperfect sensors, and observations that need context.

Orr Biologicals works at the boundary between biology in motion and the people trying to understand it. The projects below are research concepts in development. Their visualizations are synthetic and their claims stay proportional to the evidence available.
