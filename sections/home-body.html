Orr Biologicals is an open-source biotechnology platform: scientific literature and honest protocols, practical experiments anyone can repeat, real-world applications weighed against their evidence, and original engineering — from a culture on your bench to the instruments built to study it. The goal never changes: make biology more understandable, accessible and reproducible.

I–01

Algaephyte

Open-source systems for understanding and monitoring microalgal cultures — sensors, a camera and a physics-informed model, with hard limits and an honest log. Illustrative scene of the concept at most — not a deployed instrument; no commercial deployment is claimed.

How we work

Open knowledge,
or it doesn’t count.

Biology is a public good. Everything on this site — code, protocols, build scripts and corrections — is MIT-licensed: readable, reusable, forkable. When a claim is uncertain, the uncertainty is printed next to it, not hidden under marketing.

Algaephyte and Cyanoflow are our only inventions; everything else is open reference material. If a page is hard to use with your setup, that is a bug — write to us.
